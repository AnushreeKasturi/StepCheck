# Algebra practice sheet

Thanks for helping with my project! It checks handwritten math solutions step by step.

**Please:**
- Solve each problem on paper, **showing every step on its own line**, the way you normally would.
- **Don't erase or fix mistakes.** Mistakes are exactly what I need; nobody is grading this.
- Don't use a calculator or an app. Skipping a problem is fine.
- When done, take one photo per problem: in good light, from directly above, whole page visible.

Your name is not stored. Photos are only used to test the project and are never published.
